# Privacy Policy

**AI Bulk Chat Manager** (the "extension")
Last updated: 2026-11-04

AI Bulk Chat Manager helps you select, delete, archive and export your chats on supported AI chat sites.

## In short

- It works locally on your device. It has no server and sends your data nowhere.
- No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
- It does not collect, sell or share personal data.

## What it reads

- The list of your chats (titles and links) on the page you are on, while you select chats.
- The text of a chat, only when you click an export button, and only to make your file on your device. It does not analyze or understand that text, and it does not keep it.

## What it stores

Your settings and a small reminder timer, in your browser only. Reset them in Options, or remove everything by uninstalling the extension.

## Requests

When you delete, archive or export, the extension asks the chat site you are on, using your own signed-in session. Nothing is sent to anyone else.

## Permissions

The extension needs access to the supported chat sites to add checkboxes and carry out your actions. It also uses `storage` for your settings, and `activeTab` and `scripting` to start on the page you choose. It uses them only as described here.

## Links

When you install or remove the extension, and when you click its guide, support or feedback links, a page on enaloo.com or GitHub opens. These addresses may carry your extension version, language, browser and system type, so the page can show the right content.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from browser APIs follows the Chrome Web Store [User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the Limited Use requirements. It is used only for the features described here, and it is not transferred, sold or used for advertising.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at <https://github.com/ehsanenaloo/AI-Bulk-Chat-Manager/issues> and do not include personal data. For anything sensitive, use the private reporting process in [SECURITY.md](../.github/SECURITY.md).
